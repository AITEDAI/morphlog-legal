Frequently asked questions

Which devices are supported?

MorphLog requires an iPhone with a TrueDepth camera (Face ID) — iPhone X or later — running iOS 17 or later. 3D face analysis does not work on devices without a TrueDepth sensor.

Are my face photos uploaded to a server?

No. Photos, 3D meshes, and analysis results are stored only on your device and are never sent to an external server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

My face isn't being detected properly.

  • Adjust your distance so your face fits inside the guide circle.
  • Face the camera directly and keep your head angle steady.
  • Avoid very dark rooms and backlighting; use even lighting.
  • Remove hats, masks, and sunglasses.
  • Capturing at the same time and place each day makes comparisons more accurate.

Health data isn't syncing.

Check that read access is enabled under Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > MorphLog on your iPhone. Health integration is optional — all core features work without it.

How do I manage my Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account. Go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone to change or cancel at any time. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current billing period ends. If you changed devices or reinstalled the app, use Settings > Restore Purchases inside the app.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete all data from Settings > Delete All Data in the app. Deleting the app also removes everything stored on your device. Because data lives only on your device, deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Will my records transfer to a new device?

All data is stored locally, so please use a full device backup and restore via iCloud or iTunes when switching devices.

Is MorphLog a medical device?

No. MorphLog is intended for recording and reference only, not for medical diagnosis or treatment. Please consult a healthcare professional for any health-related concerns.
